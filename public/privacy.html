LetsLiinger — Privacy Policy

Effective August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what LetsLiinger collects, why, and your choices. LetsLiinger is a campus social app for students to discover, post, and RSVP to events and connect with classmates.

1. Information we collect

  • Account info: your email address (used to sign in and secure your account).
  • Profile info: display name, username, bio, school, major/minor, graduation year, interests, clubs, and a profile photo you choose to upload.
  • Content you create: events, RSVPs, likes, comments, and direct messages.
  • Location: if you allow it, approximate location is used to show events near you and place event pins on the map. You can decline; the app still works.
  • Photos: if you set a profile picture or event cover image, we access the photo you select.
  • Basic technical data needed to operate the app (e.g. sign-in sessions).

2. How we use your information

To run the app: to sign you in, show your profile to other students, display events and the map, deliver messages, and keep the community safe (moderation, blocking, and reviewing reports).

3. What we share

Your profile, events, comments, and public activity are visible to other users of LetsLiinger. Direct messages are visible to the people in the conversation. We use Supabase to host our database, authentication, and file storage on our behalf. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data linked to you

The data above (email, profile, location, photos, and content) is linked to your account identity.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time.
  • Turn off location permission in your device settings.
  • Block and report other users.
  • Permanently delete your account and data from Settings → Delete Account.

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR / CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete your data. Email letsliinger@gmail.com to make a request.

6. Children

LetsLiinger is not directed to children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to use it.

7. Changes

We may update this Policy and will notify you in the app of material changes.

8. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email letsliinger@gmail.com.

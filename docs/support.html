Common questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app, tap Create Account, and sign up with your email. You'll pick your campus during setup so you see events from your school.

How do I post or RSVP to an event?

Tap + Create Event on the home screen to post one (add a photo, location, date, and category). To attend an event, open it and tap RSVP — you can also add guests or join the waitlist if it's full.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the person's profile, tap the ••• menu, and choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed by our team, and blocking hides your profile and messages from that person in both directions. You can also report an event from the event's menu.

How do I manage notifications?

Go to Settings → Notification settings to choose which notifications you receive (messages, follows, new events, and more).

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and associated data (profile, events, RSVPs, messages). This cannot be undone.

I found a bug or have feedback

Please email letsliinger@gmail.com with a description and, if possible, a screenshot. It really helps us improve.
